Life often places us in unexpected "wells" — moments of isolation, betrayal, hardship, and deep emotional trial. Yet, in the story of Prophet Yusuf (’alayhis salam), Allah (Subhanahu wa Ta’ala) shows us how the deepest wells can become the starting point for spiritual elevation, resilience, and ultimate victory.

Surviving the Well is a 4-part journey through Surah Yusuf, designed specifically for women seeking to navigate life’s personal trials through the wisdom of the Qur’an. Together, we will examine the thematic depth of Ahsan al-Qasas (“The Best of Stories”), drawing actionable spiritual tools to heal, remain steadfast, and cultivate beautiful patience (*Sabrun Jameel*).
