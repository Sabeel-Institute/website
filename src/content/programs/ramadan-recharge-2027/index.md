## Ramadan Recharge 2027: Maximizing Your Ramadan

Ramadan is more than a month of fasting, it is an opportunity to strengthen our relationship with Allah, purify our hearts, and transform our lives.

Join Sabeel Institute for a six-class journey exploring the virtues and fiqh of fasting, developing love for Allah, the power of duʿāʾ, spiritual growth, and practical ways to make the most of Ramadan.

Come away with the knowledge, inspiration, and a practical plan to make this Ramadan your most meaningful yet!
