Prepare your heart and mind for Ramadan with Sabeel’s upcoming daily Tafseer program! Beginning prior to the holy month on February 2nd and concluding on March 6th, join our teachers and guest speakers as we journey together through the Qur’an.

## Program highlights

- **Daily Selected Passages:** Every day from 12:30 PM–1:30 PM CT, our instructors and guest speakers will cover the Tafseer and key themes of selected passages from the Juz of the day.
- **Milestone Reflection Sessions:** After completing every 10 Ajza, we will host a special reflection session to review, connect, and synthesize lessons from the past 10 Juz.

## Flexible attendance options

- **Online:** Available every day live from the comfort of your home.
- **On-site:** Join us in person on Tuesdays and Saturdays (also streamed online).

**Format:** Daily Online | On-site on Tuesdays & Saturdays

Join us as we reflect on the divine words and draw meaningful guidance for our daily lives!
