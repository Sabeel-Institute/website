A journey through folktales from around the world! Through diverse storytelling traditions, imaginative play, nature-inspired crafts, and hands-on activities, children will explore timeless lessons of kindness, honesty, courage, gratitude, and compassion.

Rooted in Waldorf-inspired learning and enriched with Islamic teachings, each story becomes an opportunity to nurture creativity, cultivate beautiful character (*akhlaq*), and strengthen their connection with Allah (SWT).

## Weekly themes & activities

### Week 1 — Syria
- **Story:** The Woodcutter & the Lion
- **Story Style:** Shadow Theater
- **Activity:** Forest of Ihsan (felt mosaic art)
- **Explore:** Kindness • Friendship • Gratitude
- **Country Table:** Learn about Syria through immersive exploration

### Week 2 — Ethiopia
- **Story:** The Dog Fight
- **Story Style:** Story board Role-Play
- **Activity:** Make own color + Watercolor River Art
- **Explore:** Resolving Conflicts • Patience
- **Country Table:** Learn about Ethiopia through immersive exploration

### Week 3 — Sudan
- **Story:** Allah Karim
- **Story Style:** Interactive Storytelling
- **Activity:** Fall soup
- **Explore:** Generosity • Forgiveness
- **Country Table:** Learn about Sudan through immersive exploration

### Week 4 — Palestine
- **Story:** True Kindness
- **Story Style:** Oral Storytelling & Traditional Games (Al Sab’a Balata)
- **Activity:** Patterned Bookmark Leather Craft
- **Explore:** Kindness • Giving
- **Country Table:** Learn about Palestine through immersive exploration

### Week 5 — Yemen
- **Story:** The Next Sultan
- **Story Style:** Dramatic Storytelling & Imaginative Play
- **Activity:** Yemeni chai making and traditional Yemeni game (Min Kobet Al-Tayyar)
- **Explore:** Responsibility • Leadership
- **Country Table:** Learn about Yemen through immersive exploration

### Week 6 — Afghanistan
- **Story:** The Emir & the Angel
- **Story Style:** Puppet Theater
- **Activity:** Weaving and Justice sock Puppets
- **Explore:** Justice • Accountability
- **Country Table:** Learn about Afghanistan through immersive exploration
